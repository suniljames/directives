# Healthcare Domain Overlay

Additions for projects operating under HIPAA (the US Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) or similar healthcare privacy regulation.

> **Not legal advice.** These addenda are an educational starting point distilled from one production system. Following them does not make you HIPAA-compliant — have qualified counsel review your compliance posture.

Apply these on top of the base directives by referencing them from your project's `CONTRIBUTING.md` (see [Getting Started → Adding domain overlays](../../docs/getting-started.md#adding-domain-overlays)):
